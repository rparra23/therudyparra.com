Long bio (~120 words)

Rudy Parra is an electrical lab technician and embedded systems developer building clean-energy hardware in Albuquerque, New Mexico. At GridFlow he specializes in vanadium redox flow batteries, tackling long-duration energy storage that gives commercial customers energy resiliency. As lead software developer at Pii Energy he builds the full-stack platform behind a direct-to-consumer plug-in solar hybrid system that delivers peak shifting and energy cost savings. A former researcher at the National Solar Observatory and Lockheed Martin, he is first author on a NASA/NSF-funded solar spectroscopy poster and a co-author on published work in glass physics, X-ray absorption spectroscopy, and plasma science. Off the bench, Rudy serves on the Board of NM Tech Talks, co-organizes the Desert Dev Lab hackathon, directs the New Mexico chapter of Techqueria, and founded Q-STEM, a meetup for LGBTQ+ folks in STEM.
