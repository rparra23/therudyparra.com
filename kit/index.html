Long bio (~120 words)

Rudy Parra is an electrical lab technician and embedded systems developer building clean-energy hardware in Albuquerque, New Mexico. At GridFlow he specializes in long-duration energy storage for commercial use, working on vanadium redox flow battery systems. As lead software developer at Pii Energy — one of the first companies bringing plug-in solar to the U.S. — he built the full-stack platform behind the Edge 2000, the first affordable plug-in solar generation system for American homes. A former researcher at the National Solar Observatory and Lockheed Martin, he is first author on a NASA/NSF-funded solar spectroscopy poster and a co-author on published work in glass physics, X-ray absorption spectroscopy, and plasma science. Off the bench, Rudy serves on the Board of NM Tech Talks, co-organizes the Desert Dev Lab hackathon, and directs the New Mexico chapter of Techqueria.
