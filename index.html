I

Profile

I work in tech startups. At GridFlow I'm a lab technician specializing in vanadium redox flow batteries — tackling long-duration energy storage for commercial use and providing energy resiliency. At Pii Energy I'm the lead software developer, building a plug-in solar hybrid system sold direct to consumers for peak shifting and energy cost savings. Off the bench, I serve on the Board of NM Tech Talks, direct the New Mexico chapter of Techqueria, and founded Q-STEM, a meetup for LGBTQ+ folks in STEM. I build things that bridge electrons, atoms, and code.
