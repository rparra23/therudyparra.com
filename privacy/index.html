Privacy

This is my personal site — here's everything it does with your data, in plain language.

No cookies, no trackers

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies and uses no third-party analytics. I count page views with a first-party counter that stores only "this page got a visit" — no IP addresses, no identifiers, no profiles. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests the font files from Google's servers like on most of the web.

What I collect — only if you give it

If you fill out the "We met?" form or sign up for my newsletter, I store exactly what you type (name, contact, your note, or your email) so I can follow up or send you the newsletter. That data lives in my own storage, is never sold or shared, and I'll delete yours anytime — just ask.

Newsletter

You'll only get it if you signed up, and every email includes a way to unsubscribe. Email me and I'll remove you immediately either way.

Questions or deletions
