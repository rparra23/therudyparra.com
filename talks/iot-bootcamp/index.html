Day job, part one
GridFlow
Senior Electrical Lab Technician, working on vanadium redox flow batteries for long duration storage.
What a flow battery is
Liquid electrolyte in tanks instead of solid cells. Scale the tanks, scale the hours of storage.
What I do
ESP32 and Particle Photon firmware in C++. Current, voltage, temp, and GPS sensors over BLE and Wi-Fi. Python on the DAQ side.
Why it matters
Commercial energy resiliency. Keeping the lights on when the grid cannot.
Day job, part two
Pii Energy
Lead Software Developer on the Edge 2000, a plug-in solar and battery system for regular people. Launched Pi Day 2025. Featured in PV Magazine.
This is IoT
Hardware in someone's house, reporting state every minute, controlled from an app.
The stack
Device telemetry into AWS: API Gateway in front of Lambda. Room-level outlet control and overcurrent detection in the app.
The hard part
Not the code. Making it simple enough that nobody needs an electrician to use it.
Nobody hired me for the thing I studied
Solar physics did not lead to solar hardware on purpose. The bootcamp did not teach me flow batteries. Every job I have came from being curious out loud and knowing somebody.
